# Design System: DARSH Cinematic 3D Portfolio

## 1. Brand Identity
- **Name:** DARSH
- **Logo Concept:** A refractive, glass-morphic "D" monolith that acts as a lens for the underlying project data.
- **Atmosphere:** High-end, studio-grade, minimalist, dark-mode.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Deep Space:** `#000000` (Core background)
- **Obsidian:** `#050508` (Section depth)
- **Cyan Glow:** `#00f5ff` (Technical highlights / Active states)
- **Glass White:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` (HUD elements)

## 3. Typography
- **Headlines:** 'Inter', sans-serif (Bold, tracking-tighter)
- **Technical:** 'IBM Plex Mono', monospace (Light, uppercase, tracking-widest)

## 4. 3D & Motion Principles
- **Cylindrical Gallery:** A 3D cylinder rotating on the Y-axis, mapped to scroll progression.
- **Parallax:** Multi-layered background grids moving at different speeds.
- **Inertia:** GSAP-driven smooth scrolling with high friction for a "heavy" premium feel.

## 5. UI Components
- **HUD (Heads-Up Display):** Fixed overlay with coordinate counters and archive labels.
- **Glass Cards:** Project cards with subtle refraction and border glows.
